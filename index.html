Frequently asked questions

Everything you
need to know.

Clear answers regarding check-in times, room rates, complimentary breakfast, and guest policies at De Palms Hotels.

What are the room categories, nightly rates, and breakfast inclusions? +

Our official room rates per night are:

  • Mini Deluxe Room: ₦60,000 / night (Note: No complimentary breakfast)
  • Super Deluxe Room: ₦90,000 / night (Includes complimentary breakfast)
  • Royal Room: ₦110,000 / night (Includes complimentary breakfast)
  • Special Room: ₦130,000 / night (Includes complimentary breakfast)
  • Ambassadorial Room: ₦150,000 / night (Includes complimentary breakfast)
  • Presidential Room: ₦250,000 / night (Includes complimentary breakfast)

All rooms include high-speed Wi-Fi, air conditioning, smart TV with Netflix, hot showers, and full access to our swimming pool.

What time is check-in and check-out? +

Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM, and check-out is strictly at 12:00 PM (Noon). Early check-in before 2:00 PM is subject to room availability upon arrival. Check-in before 5:00 AM without prior notice maintains a checkout time of 12:00 PM on that same day. Late checkout may be requested at reception at least 3 hours prior to departure, subject to availability.

Is smoking allowed in guest rooms? +

No. De Palms Hotels maintains a 100% smoke-free policy across all guest rooms, suites, and interior corridors. Smoking (including cigarettes, cigars, vapes, and shisha) is strictly prohibited indoors. For the comfort of smokers, dedicated open-air zones are available at our Bush Bar and courtyard gardens.

What are the visitor hours and security guidelines? +

All non-resident visitors must register at the front desk with a valid government-issued photo ID before accessing guest room floors. For guest security and peaceful rest, non-resident visitors are not permitted in rooms after 10:00 PM. Only registered guests are allowed overnight.

What payment methods are accepted for reservations? +

We accept verified electronic bank transfers, Point of Sale (POS) debit/credit cards, and verified corporate payment accounts. Reservations are guaranteed upon confirmed payment.

What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy? +

Reservations cancelled or adjusted at least 24 hours prior to standard check-in time may be rescheduled without penalty, subject to availability. Same-day cancellations or no-shows are non-refundable.

What are the swimming pool and leisure facility hours? +

Our courtyard swimming pool is open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM for resident hotel guests and authorized visitors. Proper swimwear is required, glassware is prohibited poolside, and children under 14 must be supervised by an adult.
