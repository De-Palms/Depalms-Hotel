Hotel Guidelines & Guest Information

Policies &
House Rules.

To ensure a calm, comfortable and seamless stay for all our guests, please take a moment to review our house policies and guidelines.

01 / Check-In Special Policy

Check-In & Early Morning Arrival

We strive to make arrival effortless and smooth:

  • Standard Check-In: Check-in begins at 2:00 PM. Early check-in before 2:00 PM is subject to room availability upon arrival.
  • Early Check-In Before 5:00 AM: Check-in before 5:00 AM without prior notice will still maintain a checkout time of 12:00 PM on that same day. Guests wishing to extend their stay through the night should arrange with the front desk in advance.
  • Identification: A valid government-issued photo ID (Passport, Driver's License, or National ID) is required at registration for all resident guests.
02 / Check-Out Strict 12:00 PM

Check-Out & Departures

To allow our housekeeping team adequate time to prepare rooms for arriving guests:

  • Checkout Time: Standard checkout time is strictly 12:00 PM (Noon).
  • Late Checkout: Late departures must be requested at reception at least 3 hours prior to checkout. Extensions are subject to room availability and may attract a nominal late checkout fee.
  • Key Card Return: Please surrender all room key cards at the front desk upon departure.
03 / Clean Air Strictly Enforced

No Smoking in Rooms

For the health, safety, and comfort of all hotel residents:

  • 100% Smoke-Free Rooms: Smoking of any kind—including cigarettes, cigars, electronic cigarettes (vapes), and shisha—is strictly prohibited inside all guest rooms, suites, and interior corridors.
  • Designated Smoking Areas: Smoking is permitted exclusively in designated open-air outdoor zones, such as the Bush Bar and courtyard gardens.
  • Sanitation Fee: Any guest found smoking in a room will be assessed a mandatory deep-cleaning and air-ionization restoration charge.
04 / Visitors Safety First

Visitor Registration & Access

We take guest safety and property security seriously:

  • Reception Sign-In: All non-resident visitors must register at the front desk before proceeding to residential floors.
  • Visiting Hours: For the tranquility and privacy of resident guests, visitors are not permitted in guest rooms after 10:00 PM.
  • Overnight Occupancy: Only registered guests are permitted overnight stay. Unregistered overnight visitors will incur supplementary room occupancy rates.
05 / Atmosphere 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM

Serenity & Quiet Hours

De Palms is designed for calm, restful stays:

  • Quiet Hours: Observed daily between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM. Please maintain television, media, and conversation in rooms and hallways at a considerate volume.
  • No Parties: Unauthorized parties, loud gatherings, and disruptive noise inside guest rooms are strictly prohibited.
  • Management reserves the right to decline or terminate service to anyone engaging in conduct that breaches the comfort and peace of other patrons.
06 / Property Room Care

Care of Property & Inventory

Every space has been thoughtfully outfitted for your convenience:

  • Room Furnishings: Guests are kindly asked to treat all room electronics, furnishings, artworks, and linens with due care.
  • Damages & Missing Amenities: Any accidental or willful damage to hotel property, or missing inventory (towels, bathrobes, remote controls, fixtures), will be billed to the guest's folio at full replacement value.
07 / Facilities 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Swimming Pool & Leisure Spaces

Enjoy our courtyard pool and lifestyle comforts safely:

  • Access: The swimming pool is reserved for resident hotel guests and authorized club visitors.
  • Safety & Glassware: No glassware or breakables are allowed in or immediately around the pool water. Proper swimwear is required.
  • Children: Children under the age of 14 must be accompanied and supervised by a responsible adult at all times while at the pool area.
08 / Bookings Reservations

Payments & Booking Terms

Clear, straightforward reservation processes:

  • Payment: Reservations are guaranteed upon confirmed payment. We accept verified electronic bank transfers, Point of Sale (POS) debit/credit cards, and verified corporate accounts.
  • Cancellations: Cancellations or adjustments made at least 24 hours prior to standard check-in time may be rescheduled without penalty, subject to room availability.
  • No-Shows: Same-day cancellations or failure to arrive without prior notification are non-refundable.
Questions regarding our policies or have special requests?

Our front desk team is on duty 24 hours a day to assist with early check-ins, airport transfers, or custom arrangements.
